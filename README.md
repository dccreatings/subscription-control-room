# Subscription Control Room

A personal subscription tracker: add every subscription you pay for, see your true monthly and yearly totals, and pause/resume them in your own list.

Everything lives entirely in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent to a server, and no account or bank connection is required.

## Features

- Add, edit, and delete subscriptions (name, cost, billing cycle, category, next renewal date)
- Pause/resume a subscription to exclude it from your totals without deleting it
- Automatic monthly and yearly cost totals across active subscriptions
- Search and filter (all / active / paused)
- "Manage on site" links straight to the provider's own account page, with auto-suggested links for common services (Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, etc.) — since this app can't cancel a subscription for you, it takes you straight to the page that can

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's it — no install, no server, no build step.

## Note on "unsubscribing"

This app does not have the ability to actually cancel a subscription with the provider — no personal app does, that requires a direct business relationship with each company. Pausing a subscription here only updates your own tracking list. Use the "Manage on site" link to actually cancel with the provider.
